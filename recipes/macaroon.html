Macaroon

Home

This is a sweet and chewy macaroon recipe that is perfect to impress any guests that come knocking at your door!

Ingredients

Macaroon Shells

  • 105 gram egg white, aged
  • 144 gram almond flour
  • 105 gram granulated sugar
  • 125 gram powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp food coloring

Macaroon Filling

  • 113 gram unsalted butter, softened
  • 140 gram powdered sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Separate the egg whites into a pull and let them age uncovered at room temperature for 24 hours.
  2. Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper or specialized macaroon mats.
  3. Sift the almond flour and powdered sugar together to remove any lumps into adry-ingredient bowl.
  4. In a stand mixer, mix the egg whites until they foam and then add your granulated sugar.
  5. Whip for at least 6 minutes to form stiff peaks of your meringue.
  6. Add any desired food coloring to the meringue at this stage.
  7. Remove from the mixer and sift in your almound flour mixture into the meringue.
  8. Take a spatula and fold gently utnil the mixture comes off the spatula in ribbons
  9. Transfer the batter into a piping bag fitted with a round tip and pipe small rounds onto the prepared baking sheets.
  10. Tap the baking sheets on the counter to release any air bubbles and let them sit at room temperature for 30-60 minutes to form a skin.
  11. Bake in a preheated oven at 325°F for 10-12 minutes, then let them cool completely on the baking sheets.
  12. Prepare the filling by beating the softened butter, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract in the stand mixer until smooth and creamy.
  13. Once the macaroon shells are cooled, pipe the filling onto one shell and sandwich with another shell.
  14. Repeat the process until all macaroon shells are filled and sandwiched.
  15. Store the macaroons in the refrigerator for at least 24 hours to allow the flavors to meld together before serving.
  16. Serve the macaroons chilled and enjoy!
