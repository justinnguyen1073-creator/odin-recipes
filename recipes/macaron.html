Macaron

This is a sweet and chewy macaron recipe that is perfect for impressing any guests who come knocking at your door!

  • Oven
  • 325°F
  • Plan two days ahead

Inspired by Nick DiGiovanni.

Steps

  1. Separate the egg whites into a bowl and let them age uncovered at room temperature for 24 hours.
  2. Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper or macaron mats.
  3. Sift the almond flour and powdered sugar together into a bowl to remove any lumps.
  4. In a stand mixer, whisk the egg whites until they foam, then add your granulated sugar.
  5. Whip for at least 6 minutes until the meringue forms stiff peaks.
  6. Add any food coloring you like to the meringue at this stage.
  7. Remove the bowl from the mixer and sift your almond flour mixture into the meringue.
  8. Fold gently with a spatula until the batter falls off the spatula in ribbons.
  9. Transfer the batter into a piping bag fitted with a round tip and pipe small rounds onto the prepared baking sheets.
  10. Tap the baking sheets on the counter to release any air bubbles, then let them sit at room temperature for 30 to 60 minutes to form a skin.
  11. Bake in a preheated oven at 325°F for 10 to 12 minutes, then let them cool completely on the baking sheets.
  12. Make the filling by beating the softened butter, powdered sugar and vanilla extract in the stand mixer until smooth and creamy.
  13. Once the shells are cool, pipe the filling onto one shell and sandwich it with another.
  14. Repeat until all the shells are filled and sandwiched.
  15. Store the macarons in the refrigerator for at least 24 hours so the flavors meld together.
  16. Serve the macarons chilled and enjoy!
