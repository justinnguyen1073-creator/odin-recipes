Slow cooker tacos

Birria Tacos

Beef simmers all day in a smoky chipotle and tomato broth. Then you dip the tortillas in that broth, crisp them up with cheese, and keep the rest for dunking.

  • Slow cooker
  • 8 hours on low
  • 8 servings

Based on Slow Cooker Birria Tacos by Gina Homolka at Skinnytaste.

Steps

  1. Brown the beef on every side in a hot skillet, working in two batches, then move it into the slow cooker.
  2. Blend 1 cup of the onion with the garlic, chipotles, crushed tomatoes, broth, vinegar and all of the spices until smooth.
  3. Pour the sauce over the beef, drop in the bay leaves, and cook on low for 8 hours.
  4. Shred the beef with two forks. Strain the broth, then put the beef back in the slow cooker with 1/2 cup of the broth to keep it warm.
  5. Save the rest of the strained broth. This is the consommé for dipping.
  6. Heat a skillet or griddle. Dip a tortilla in the consommé and lay it in the pan.
  7. Top one half with about 3 tbsp of beef, 2 tbsp of cheese and a little onion and cilantro, then fold it over.
  8. Cook until both sides are browned and crispy, then repeat with the rest.
  9. Serve with more onion and cilantro, lime wedges and a small bowl of warm consommé on the side for dunking.

Good to know

  • Short on time? Cook on high for 4 to 5 hours instead of low for 8.
  • Leftover beef and consommé keep in the fridge for up to 4 days, or in the freezer for up to 3 months.
