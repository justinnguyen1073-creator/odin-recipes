Chicken Alfredo

Home

This is a creamy and delicious chicken alfredo recipe that is perfect for meal-prep.

Ingredients

  • 2 whole chicken breasts, diced
  • 700g pasta
  • 800g cottage cheese
  • 120g low-fat cream cheese
  • 240 ml milk
  • 150g Parmigiano Reggiano
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp garlic puree
  • Spice blend

Spice blend

  • 2 tsp kinder garlic butter seasoning (optional)
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • dash of paprika
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp of black pepper

Steps

  1. In a slow cooker, add the diced chicken breast, 30g of Parmigiano Reggiano, and a juice of a lemon.
  2. Add garlic puree, the spice blend, and 1/2 cup of water and mix till evenly combined.
  3. Evenly distribute the butter over the mixture and let cook on low for 5-6 hours, or until the chicken is fully cooked.
  4. Once the chicken is cooked, shred it using two forks and add in half-cooked pasta and your blended alfredo sauce (see recipe below)
  5. Mix everything together until well combined.
  6. Continue cooking the mixture on low for an additional 30 minutes, or until the pasta is fully cooked and the sauce has thickened.
  7. Dish out the chicken alfredo into 10 servings and enjoy!

Low-Calorie Alfredo Sauce

Blend together these ingredients:

  • 800g cottage cheese
  • 120g low-fat cream cheese
  • 240 ml milk
  • 120g Parmigiano Reggiano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
