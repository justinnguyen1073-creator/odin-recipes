Chicken Alfredo

This is a creamy and delicious chicken alfredo recipe that is perfect for meal prep.

  • Slow cooker
  • 10 servings

Inspired by @stealth_health_life on TikTok.

Ingredients

Tick things off as you gather them.

Spice blend

Steps

  1. In a slow cooker, add the diced chicken breast, 30g of Parmigiano Reggiano and the juice of the lemon.
  2. Add the garlic puree, the spice blend and 1/2 cup of water, and mix until evenly combined.
  3. Spread the butter evenly over the mixture and cook on low for 5 to 6 hours, or until the chicken is fully cooked.
  4. Once the chicken is cooked, shred it with two forks, then add the half-cooked pasta and your blended alfredo sauce (see below).
  5. Mix everything together until well combined.
  6. Keep cooking on low for another 30 minutes, or until the pasta is fully cooked and the sauce has thickened.
  7. Divide the chicken alfredo into 10 servings and enjoy!

Low-calorie alfredo sauce

Blend these ingredients together until smooth:

  • 800g cottage cheese
  • 120g low-fat cream cheese
  • 240 ml milk
  • 120g Parmigiano Reggiano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
