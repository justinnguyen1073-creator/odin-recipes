Feta Salmon Pasta

This is a delicious pasta dish made with feta cheese and salmon. Perfect for a quick and tasty dinner!

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 150g feta cheese
  • 200g salmon fillet
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a baking dish, add your salmonf filet, block of feta, clove of garlic, and drizzle with olive oil.
  2. Bake in the oven at 375°F for 30 minutes.
  3. Remove the baking dish from the oven.
  4. Squeeze the roasted garlic out of the skin
  5. Mix until all ingredients are combined.
  6. Add in your pasta cooked according to the package instructions and mix well.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
