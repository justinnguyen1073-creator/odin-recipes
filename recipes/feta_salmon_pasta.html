Feta Salmon Pasta

This is a delicious pasta dish made with feta cheese and salmon. Perfect for a quick and tasty dinner!

  • Oven
  • 375°F
  • 30 min bake

Ingredients

Tick things off as you gather them.

Steps

  1. In a baking dish, add your salmon fillet, block of feta and clove of garlic, then drizzle with olive oil.
  2. Bake in the oven at 375°F for 30 minutes.
  3. Remove the baking dish from the oven.
  4. Squeeze the roasted garlic out of its skin.
  5. Mix until all the ingredients are combined.
  6. Add your pasta, cooked according to the package instructions, and mix well.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
